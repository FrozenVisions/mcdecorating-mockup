Finish, light, and a wall that should wait

Light across Tyrone and Fermanagh is flat for much of the year. A colour picked from a phone often looks duller on a north-facing wall than it did on the screen. If you are unsure, put a sample on the actual wall and look at it in the morning and again in the evening before the whole room is committed. Late autumn is a useful week for that check, because you are seeing the light the room has for most of the year.

The interior painting page names matt for walls, and a wipeable eggshell or satin for doors, skirting and banisters, because hands touch them. A dark colour going pale, or a water stain, takes a primer and more coats, and that belongs in the quote. If the paint is already bought, give the name of the tin and say whether you are supplying it. A lower figure with the paint left out is a different job.

A patch that is still damp is a reason to wait. Mould and condensation marks come back if the cause is still feeding them, and ordinary emulsion will not stop that.
