A finish for the parts people touch

Doors, frames and the lower walls take the knocks. Ask for a hard-wearing finish there, in general terms: matt on the upper wall, and a wipeable eggshell or satin where hands touch. A dark colour going pale, or a stain, takes a primer and more coats, and that belongs in the quote. If you are supplying the paint, name the tin.
