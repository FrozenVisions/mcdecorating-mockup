Painter and decorator in Fintona

Fintona is a short drive south of Omagh, close enough that a hallway and a living room is a normal job rather than a long day out.

Get a free quote Call 07907 588 541

Fintona sits about a quarter of an hour south of Omagh. The work is village houses on and off the main street, bungalows, and older rendered homes out the Seskinore and Trillick roads. It is not a place that needs a decorator to make a special expedition from Belfast. Martin is already based up the road.

A typical ask is practical rather than a full show house: paper above the dado in the hall, woodwork that has yellowed, a ceiling that needs filled before it is painted, and a living room that has gone tired. Older rendered walls on the edge of the village are the ones to look at before any exterior coat. If the render is soft or the wall is damp, paint is the wrong first step.

Streets in the village are tight, so dust sheets and a tidy path matter more than they do on an open estate. Furniture gets covered, and the job is left so the house can be lived in at the end of the day. Seskinore and Trillick, between Fintona and the main roads, are the same run. If that is you, say the townland on the quote form. You do not need a page of your own to be on the round.
