Painter and decorator in Carrickmore

Carrickmore is east of Omagh on the way to Cookstown. Village houses, bungalows and farmhouses, close enough to share a day with other jobs on that side.

Get a free quote Call 07907 588 541

Carrickmore, and the country around Termon, is the east side of Omagh. It is about a quarter of an hour out, which puts it in the same class as Fintona and Gortin: near enough that you do not need a whole-house brief to get a quote. Bungalows, village houses and farmhouses are the stock of work. A full interior before a house is sold comes up as well.

The decorating itself is familiar. Halls with paper over a dado, ceilings that need filling, and woodwork included so the room does not look half finished. If the house is in a townland rather than the village, say which. Beragh and Sixmilecross, back towards Omagh on this same side, are covered even though they do not have their own pages. So is the road on towards Cookstown.

Carrickmore is also the sensible stop on the way to mid-Ulster. A job here can sit beside a larger one further east, which keeps the travel fair. You will still get a quote for Carrickmore on its own.
