What Martin does

From one room to a whole property, inside or out. The photographs are recent jobs. They are houses, not a shop display.

Interior painting

Rooms, halls, stairs and woodwork, from a single wall to a whole house. Martin Kelly quotes the job in front of him. There is no set price list.

Exterior painting

Render, pebbledash, fascias, doors and windows for houses and small businesses. The coat only lasts if the wall is ready and the week is dry enough.

Wallpapering

From one feature wall to a hallway and stairs. The paper only looks right if the wall under it is sound, dry and flat.

Ceiling and wall repairs

Cracks, holes and scarred plaster made good so paint and paper have a flat, sound surface. This is preparation for decorating, not structural building work.
